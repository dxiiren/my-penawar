About the clinic

Serving a better hospice

Owned by Kumpulan Perubatan Penawar Sdn. Bhd. and founded by Dr. Rushmini Maris Ismail, Poliklinik Penawar contributes to the care of patients suffering from behavioural issues — especially those from low socio-economic backgrounds.

  • One-stop specialists' centre with personalised patient care
  • Mental-health services and community outreach
  • Nutritional and dietary planning
